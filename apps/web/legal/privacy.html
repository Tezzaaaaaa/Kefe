KEFE Visualiser ("KEFE") is a browser-based lyric video and music visualiser editor operated by [PLACEHOLDER: legal name or business name, ABN if any], Queensland, Australia. Contact: [PLACEHOLDER: email address].

The short version

KEFE runs in your browser. Your audio, video, lyrics and projects are processed on your device and are not uploaded to KEFE servers. KEFE has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Some features send limited information to third-party services, listed below.

What stays on your device

Media files you load and the videos you export are handled locally in your browser. KEFE saves editor settings (such as effect and style choices) in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes them. We do not receive them.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not use cookies for tracking. We do not run advertising or analytics in the editor.

Children

KEFE is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through a third-party service via KEFE, contact us.

Your rights

We hold no personal information about you beyond what GitHub's hosting logs contain, and those are controlled by GitHub. If you want to ask us about privacy, or you are in Australia, the EU/UK or California and want to exercise a privacy right, contact us at the address above. Under Australian law you may also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes

If KEFE adds accounts, payments, cloud storage or sharing, this policy will be updated before those features launch.
