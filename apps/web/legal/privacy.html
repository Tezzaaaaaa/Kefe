KEFE Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Draft — not for commercial release. This policy is not final. The Apple-style lyrics component and production network traffic have not yet been fully audited, and an Australian privacy/legal review remains outstanding.

Who we are

KEFE Visualiser ("KEFE") is a browser-based lyric video and music visualiser editor operated by Kuresa Tereroa Afamasaga, an individual in Queensland, Australia. Contact: kuresa_afamasaga@yahoo.com. No business name or ABN details have been supplied for this draft.

The short version

KEFE is designed to process loaded media and projects in your browser rather than upload them to KEFE servers. KEFE has no accounts, advertising or analytics in this editor. Some features load third-party code or contact external services, as described below. This draft has not been validated against production network traffic; do not rely on it as a final account of data handling until that review is complete.

What stays on your device

Media files you load and the videos you export are handled locally in your browser. KEFE saves editor settings (such as effect and style choices) in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes them. We do not receive them.

What we do not do

The current editor is not designed to sell personal information or run advertising or analytics. This statement and the absence of tracking cookies must be confirmed against the production build and its external services before this draft is published as final.

Children

KEFE is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through a third-party service via KEFE, contact us.

Your rights

KEFE is designed not to create user accounts or store loaded media on KEFE servers. Hosting providers and third-party services may collect information as described above. You may contact us with privacy questions or requests concerning information KEFE may hold at kuresa_afamasaga@yahoo.com. If you believe the Australian Privacy Act applies and your concern is unresolved, you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes

If KEFE adds accounts, payments, cloud storage or sharing, this policy will be updated before those features launch.
